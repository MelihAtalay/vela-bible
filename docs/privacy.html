Privacy Policy – Vela

Last updated: 21 November 2026

Summary

  • Vela does not collect, store or sell personal data on our servers. We have no servers that receive your data.
  • No analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs are used. There are no ads.
  • Your highlights, notes, bookmarks, prayers, reading plans, streaks and memory verses are stored on your device.

iCloud sync

If you are signed in to iCloud, your notes, highlights, bookmarks and prayers are synced through Apple's iCloud (CloudKit) in your private iCloud database. This data is managed by Apple under your Apple Account and is not accessible to us.

Downloads and network requests

To provide content, the app connects to these services. They receive standard technical data such as your IP address, as with any web request; we do not send any personal information:

  • GitHub (github.com) – downloading additional Bible translations you choose.
  • OpenBible.com – streaming human-narrated audio of the Berean Standard Bible.
  • Hugging Face (huggingface.co) – downloading the on-device natural voice model, if you choose a natural voice.

Natural voice narration is generated entirely on your device.

Notifications

Reminders (prayer time, reading plans, memory verses) are local notifications scheduled on your device. No push data is sent to us.

Purchases

Subscriptions and purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your payment information.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

We may update this policy; the date above shows the latest version.
