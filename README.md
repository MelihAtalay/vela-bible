# Vela Bible – open data

This repository hosts the support and privacy pages of the **Vela** iOS app and the Bible text files the app downloads on demand (see Releases).

All Bible texts come from [eBible.org](https://ebible.org) and are either in the public domain or licensed under Creative Commons licenses that permit redistribution (CC BY, CC BY-SA, CC BY-ND). Texts are converted to SQLite without changing their wording. Translations with non-commercial licenses are not included.
